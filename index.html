Supply snapshot What to watch for News Treatment gap Get help

If someone may be overdosing: call 911, give naloxone (Narcan), and give rescue breaths. Give naloxone even if you think a sedative like xylazine or medetomidine is involved, since fentanyl is almost always there too. Stay with them; effects can return.

  • Any pill not handed to you by a pharmacist may be counterfeit. You can't tell by looking.
  • Test with fentanyl and xylazine test strips. Strips can miss carfentanil and nitazenes, so a negative isn't a guarantee.
  • Don't use alone. Carry naloxone, and start with a small amount.

    The treatment gap

    Medications like buprenorphine and methadone cut the risk of dying from an overdose, yet most people who need treatment don't get it. Since 2026, buprenorphine can be started by telehealth, including by phone.

    About this data
    • Overdose counts are CDC provisional predictions for rolling 12-month periods, taken from releases between June and October 2026. Each new release revises earlier months, so points from different releases don't line up exactly.
    • DEA pill figures count pills holding 2 mg or more of fentanyl. Years are calendar years except fiscal 2025. DEA reported 76% for fiscal 2023, versus 70% for calendar 2023 shown here.
    • Seizure and lab data show what police found and tested, not the whole supply. Drug-checking studies use samples people chose to send in.
    • This page is a snapshot. It doesn't update itself; the data lives in a JSON block at the top of the page's script and can be updated by hand.
